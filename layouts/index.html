R, embedded in Go.
Checked against GNU R 4.6.2, never against guesses.

go-embedded-r is in development: v0.6.0 (2026-11-06) completes milestones M0 (the oracle harness, against GNU R 4.6.2) and M1 (the parser and deparse, package syntax), identical to R's tree, tokens and deparse output on 842 of 842 files of R's base packages, and slices 1 to 3 of M2: the evaluator (package eval) has 102 of R's 210 primitives and 94 base closures, with replacement, subsetting, attributes, S3 dispatch, conditions, print, environments as values, the apply family, string functions and regular expressions, and 1 043 of 1 043 goldens match R 4.6.2. Regular expressions run on go-embedded-r/tre, a Go port of TRE with R's own quirks reproduced, and agree with R on 60 000 of 60 000 fuzz cases; grepl is still 1.44 times slower than R (an open defect). Since v0.4.0 a session is bounded by default (a breaking change): 30 000 000 steps, 16 GiB of cumulative allocation and 10 s per evaluation, with eval.Unbounded to opt out. Base, stats, regex, graphics, the notebook bridge, the playground and CRAN ports (M3 to M8) are planned. R cells are live in go-notebook v0.8.0 and playground v0.9.0: parsed and checked, not run yet. It will be a pure-Go, embeddable R interpreter for the r cells of go-notebook and its WebAssembly playground, written clean-room under the BSD-3-Clause licence.

M1 parity with R 4.6.2

The parser and deparse, judged by the oracle.

The same text is parsed by R and by Go, and the terminal tokens, the tree and the error text are compared (go run ./cmd/rparity). R's own sources are read where they are unpacked and never committed.

R is an oracle, never a source.

The sources of R are GPL-2-or-later and this project is BSD-3-Clause. No R source file, test file, help page, .Rout.save transcript or text of R's manuals is copied, and none is downloaded at run time. The golden files are the answers of R 4.6.2 to inputs written here. Contributors work from specifications: the R Language Definition, the R Internals manual, the help pages and the oracle's behaviour.
