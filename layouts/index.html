R, embedded in Go.
Checked against GNU R 4.6.2, never against guesses.

go-embedded-r is in development: v0.12.3 (2026-11-08) completes milestones M0 (the oracle harness, against GNU R 4.6.2), M1 (the parser and deparse, package syntax), identical to R's tree, tokens and deparse output on 842 of 842 files of R's base packages, and M2: the evaluator (package eval) has 175 of R's 210 primitives and 110 base closures, and 2 028 of 2 028 goldens match R 4.6.2 (9 of them recorded for macOS only); the other 35 primitives (complex, raw, S4, formula and expression types, foreign calls, .Internal) are refused with an error that says why. M3, base R rewritten clean room, is in progress: 33.9 % of base's 1 362 exports agree with R 4.6.2 (26.4 % at v0.11.0, 24.4 % at v0.10.0, 23.3 % at v0.9.0, 22.2 % at v0.8.0, 19.8 % at v0.7.0, 14.4 % at v0.6.0), through our own clean-room base library (R glue plus Go natives), string and format functions, restarts and the call stack, and environments with a one-namespace model for base (locked and active bindings; no package loading yet), and dates and times (Date, POSIXct, POSIXlt, difftime, time zones from Go's embedded IANA database). Regular expressions run on go-embedded-r/tre, a Go port of TRE with R's own quirks reproduced, and agree with R on 60 000 of 60 000 fuzz cases; grepl was 1.44 times slower than R when last measured (an open defect; tre v0.1.1 sped the engine up and the ratio is not yet re-measured). Patterns beyond tre's bounded compile limits are refused, which includes some extreme patterns R accepts; in exchange every regex bomb test stays under 256 MiB on every target, wasm included. Since v0.4.0 a session is bounded by default (a breaking change): 30 000 000 steps, 16 GiB of cumulative allocation and 10 s per evaluation, with eval.Unbounded to opt out. Stats, graphics, the notebook bridge, the playground and CRAN ports (M4 to M8) are planned. R cells are live in go-notebook v0.8.0 and playground v0.9.0: parsed and checked, not run yet. It will be a pure-Go, embeddable R interpreter for the r cells of go-notebook and its WebAssembly playground, written clean-room under the BSD-3-Clause licence.

M1 parity with R 4.6.2

The parser and deparse, judged by the oracle.

The same text is parsed by R and by Go, and the terminal tokens, the tree and the error text are compared (go run ./cmd/rparity). R's own sources are read where they are unpacked and never committed.

R is an oracle, never a source.

The sources of R are GPL-2-or-later and this project is BSD-3-Clause. No R source file, test file, help page, .Rout.save transcript or text of R's manuals is copied, and none is downloaded at run time. The golden files are the answers of R 4.6.2 to inputs written here. Contributors work from specifications: the R Language Definition, the R Internals manual, the help pages and the oracle's behaviour.
